## 🚀 How to Use LeaveTrack (5-Step Core Workflow)

### 1️⃣ Step 1: Sign In
* Open [https://leavetrack-10ut.onrender.com](https://leavetrack-10ut.onrender.com).
* Type your User ID (e.g. `EMP002` for Bob, `MG001` for Alice) and Password.
* Click **Continue**.

### 2️⃣ Step 2: Request Leave (Employee View)
* Select your **Start Date** and **End Date**.
* Toggle **Half Day** if applicable.
* Type your **Reason for absence** and click **Submit Leave Request**.
* *Note:* Saturday and Sunday weekend days are automatically excluded from your balance deduction!

### 3️⃣ Step 3: View Absence Calendar & Live Tracker
* Click the **Calendar** tab in the top navigation bar to view team absences.
* Click **Tracker** to inspect the live audit timeline of your request status.

### 4️⃣ Step 4: Approve Requests (Manager View)
* Sign in as `MG001` (Alice).
* Navigate to **Approvals** or view your **Manager Approval Queue**.
* Click **Approve** or **Reject** on team applications.

### 5️⃣ Step 5: Print HR Verification Slip
* Under **Requests**, click the **Print Slip** button next to any approved request.
* A printable verification slip will appear complete with approval badges and reference codes.
